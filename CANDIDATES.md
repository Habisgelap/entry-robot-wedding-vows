## Winner: candidate #1 (24/25)

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold,
> in sickness and in low battery, until death do us part — or until the
> next update.

Why it won, against the judge's criteria in order:

1. **On-topic joke** — unmistakably a robot wedding vow, not a generic gag.
2. **Payoff in the last few words** — "or until the next update" undercuts
   the eternal "until death do us part" with the most robot ending possible;
   the laugh lands exactly where the judge looks for it.
3. **Real vow** — the full wedding formula: "I take you", "lawfully
   wedded", "to have and to hold", "until death do us part".
4. **Specific machine side** — three distinct terms (co-processor, low
   battery, update), none of which works for a human couple.
5. **Rhythm** — it rides the classic vow cadence, so it reads aloud cleanly
   and the twist gets a clean beat.

Runner-up #2 ("out of warranty, too") has an equally good payoff but a
weaker vow frame — a promise to love "until" something, rather than the
wedding formula itself. #3's "even from your own manufacturer" is funny
but reads as a security promise, not a vow. #7's sensor joke is a gag with
barely a vow in it. #11's "I mean, rebooted" stumbles aloud.
